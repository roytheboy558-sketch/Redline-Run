      Controls and rules

      Mouse
      The crosshair follows the mouse. Left click fires, right click turns you around. Hold the crosshair against the left or right edge of the screen to look around.
      Keys
      ← → or A D steer · ↑ Space boost · ↓ brake · F fire · R turn around · C camera · H horn · P pause
      Touch
      Joystick: left or right to steer, up to boost, down to brake. Drag on the road to aim, double-tap to face forward. Gyro aim (Settings) lets you aim by turning the device.
      Gamepad
      Left stick steer, right stick aim, RT boost, X fire, RB turn around, Y camera
      Modes
      Classic: endless run. Delivery: carry a package through all 6 levels; crashes and rail hits damage it. Escape: start with 2 stars and reach the getaway chopper. Police: drive a cruiser and take down 12 suspects without hurting 5 civilians. Zombies: survive 10 waves in a tank in the ruined city.
      Turret
      At level 3 the bike or cruiser gets a twin turret that fires faster and hits twice as hard. The tank fires an explosive cannon (aim for heads) and gets an automatic sentry gun from wave 3.
      Gun
      Shoots whatever is under the crosshair. Blue on a window (2 hits take out the driver), yellow on a tire (the car loses control), white on the body (10 hits blow up a car, 16 a truck).
      Wanted
      Your first shot at a vehicle gets you 1 star. Every 20 vehicles destroyed adds another, up to 6.
      Police
      1 star: a patrol car chases and rams you. 2: two cars. 3: cops shoot. 4: roadblocks. 5: a helicopter. 6: SWAT trucks. Red arcs around the crosshair show where shots come from.
      Road
      Curves push you outward and rain loosens steering. Touching a rail costs a life, and scraping costs another every 2 seconds. Empty nitro locks until it recharges.
      Pickups
      Shield absorbs one hit, magnet pulls in coins, repair restores a life. Tuck in behind a vehicle to charge nitro from its slipstream.
      Hazards
      Barriers, rocks, driftwood and falling debris cost a life. Cones, potholes and rolling balls only slow you.
