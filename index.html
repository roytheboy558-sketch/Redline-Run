Garage and achievements

    Controls and rules
    Joystick
    Left or right to steer, up to boost, down to brake
    Keys
    ← → steer · ↑ Space boost · ↓ brake · H horn
    Camera
    C switches view · P pauses
    Gamepad
    Left stick, RT boost, Y view
    Road
    Curves push you outward and rain loosens steering. Touching a rail costs a life, and scraping costs another every 2 seconds. Empty nitro locks until it recharges.
    Power-ups
    Shield absorbs one hit, magnet pulls in coins, repair restores a life.
    Slipstream
    Tuck in close behind a vehicle to charge nitro.
    Gun
    Hold F or J (or Fire, or X on a pad) to shoot whatever is under the crosshair. It turns blue on a window (2 hits take out the driver), yellow on a tire (the car loses control) and white on the body (10 hits blow up a car, 16 a truck).
    Aim
    Drag on the screen (or with the mouse) to turn the view all the way around: ahead or behind, in first or third person. Move the crosshair onto a target and shoot. The right stick and gyro aim turn it too. Double-tap to face forward, or tap R or RB to flip front and back. Steering stays the same.
    Wanted
    Your first shot at a vehicle gets you 1 star. Every 20 vehicles destroyed adds another, up to 6.
    Police
    1 star: a patrol car chases and rams you. 2: two cars. 3: cops shoot at you. 4: roadblocks. 5: a helicopter. 6: SWAT trucks. Shoot them before they wreck you.
    Biomes
    Every level is a new world: city, desert, coast, cavern, frozen pines, then magma ridge, and the loop repeats faster.
    Hazards
    Barriers, rocks, driftwood and falling debris cost a life. Cones, potholes and rolling balls only slow you. Watch for the red warning ring before something falls.
